# Espresso Machine Maintenance

Descale the boiler every two months using a citric acid solution at roughly
fifteen grams per litre. Backflush the group head with detergent weekly, and
replace the shower screen gasket whenever the portafilter starts to sit past the
halfway mark on the lugs.

Grinder burrs are consumable. Expect to replace flat burrs after around six
hundred kilograms of coffee, sooner if the beans are especially dark and oily.
